Why I took these courses

I enrolled in these three courses because [your real motivation: career goal, business idea, curiosity about data]. Marketing decisions made on gut feeling are expensive, and I wanted to learn how to back mine with evidence.

Together the courses follow the path a customer takes. People first reach a brand on social media, the brand then tracks what they do, and finally it optimises campaigns based on what the numbers say. That is how this site is organised.

My Alison courses sit alongside MKT 4046 Marketing Analytics at UTech. Each one gave me a hands-on way to practise ideas from lectures.

Unit 1: Introduction to Marketing Analytics

Analytics exists to support management decisions, not just to produce reports. GA4 made that real: I can look at the data before choosing a page, post or campaign. [One decision you could now make with data.]

Unit 2: Segmentation, Targeting and Positioning

In class we grouped customers using RFM and cluster analysis. The Online Marketing With Google Analytics course showed a practical version: building customer segments inside Google Analytics. [A segment you would create, such as first-time visitors from TikTok versus returning visitors.]

Units 4 and 5: Customer Loyalty and Customer Lifetime Value

Retention, churn and lifetime value measure what a customer is worth over time. Assigning a value to goals in Google Analytics is the first step, because a conversion gets a dollar value I can compare across campaigns.
